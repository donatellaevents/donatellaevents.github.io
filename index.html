About

Planning and décor, under one roof

Since 2018, Donatella Events has helped families and businesses across Bengaluru bring their celebrations to life. Every occasion has its own story, and our role is to tell it beautifully.

We begin by listening. Your traditions, your taste and the feeling you want your guests to carry home guide every decision, from palette and florals to layout and light. Then we take care of the details, so you can be fully present on the day.
